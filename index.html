Accessibility

Reduces motion effects (screen shake, confetti, rain animation) for players sensitive to fast movement or on lower-powered devices - rain still affects the ball's physics either way.

Touch Controls

Swap which side the joystick and action buttons appear on, or drag them into your own layout.

While Customize Positions is on, drag the joystick or the button group (in a match) to reposition them.
